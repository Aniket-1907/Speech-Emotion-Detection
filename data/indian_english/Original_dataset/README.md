# Emotion Voice Samples Dataset 🎤

A curated collection of voice recordings categorized by emotion. Each category includes 10 English sentences voiced by multiple actors in the corresponding emotional tone.

## 📂 Directory Structure

```text
Emotion-Voice-Samples-Original/
├── Happy/ (10 sentences × many actors, total >150 samples)
├── Sad/ (10 sentences × many actors, total >150 samples)
├── Angry/ (10 sentences × many actors, total >150 samples)
└── Neutral/ (10 sentences × many actors, total >150 samples)
```



Filenames are zero-padded (e.g., `001.wav`, `002.wav`, …), and each unique sample is a distinct audio file.
